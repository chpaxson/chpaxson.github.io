# Problem
A $4 \times 4 \times h$ rectangular box contains a sphere of radius $2$ and eight smaller spheres of radius $1$.  The smaller spheres are each tangent to three sides of the box, and the larger sphere i tangent to each of the smaller spheres.  What is $h$?

answers = $2+2\sqrt{7}$, $3+2\sqrt{5}$, $4+2\sqrt{7}$, $4\sqrt{5}$, $4\sqrt{7}$

**Setting up coordinates.** Place the box with one corner at the origin, spanning $(0,0,0)$ to $(4,4,h)$. Each small sphere (radius 1) is tangent to exactly 3 faces, placing its center 1 unit from each. The 8 small sphere centers are at all combinations of $(x, y, z)$ where $x, z \in \{1, 3\}$ and $y \in \{1,\ h-1\}$.

**Large sphere position.** The large sphere (radius 2) is tangent to all four vertical faces of the $4 \times 4$ box, since $2 = 4/2$. By symmetry its center is at $(2,\ y_c,\ 2)$.

**Tangency equation.** The distance from the large sphere's center to any bottom small sphere center (e.g. $(1, 1, 1)$) must equal $r_1 + r_2 = 3$:

$$\sqrt{(2-1)^2 + (y_c - 1)^2 + (2-1)^2} = 3$$

$$\sqrt{2 + (y_c - 1)^2} = 3 \implies (y_c - 1)^2 = 7 \implies y_c = 1 + \sqrt{7}$$

**Finding $h$.** By symmetry the large sphere must be equidistant from the top and bottom layers, so $y_c = h/2$:

$$\frac{h}{2} = 1 + \sqrt{7} \implies h = \boxed{2 + 2\sqrt{7}}$$
