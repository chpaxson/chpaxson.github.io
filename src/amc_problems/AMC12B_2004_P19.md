# Problem
A truncated cone has horizontal bases with radii $18$ and $2$.  A sphere is tangent to the top, bottom, and lateral surface of the truncated cone.  What is the radius of the sphere?

answers = $6$, $4\sqrt{5}$, $9$, $10$, $6\sqrt{3}$

If we take a cross-section from the side, we will see an isosceles trapezoid with bottom $2R_1 = 36$, top $2R_2 = 4$, and an inscribed circle of radius $r$.

**Finding the height.** Since the sphere is tangent to both the top and bottom bases, its diameter equals the cone's height:
$$h = 2r$$

**Tangential condition.** A circle can be inscribed in a trapezoid if and only if the sum of opposite sides are equal:
$$\text{bottom} + \text{top} = 2 \times \text{leg}$$
$$36 + 4 = 2\,\ell \implies \ell = 20$$

**Using the Pythagorean theorem** on the right triangle formed by the slant leg, the height, and the difference in radii:
$$\ell^2 = (R_1 - R_2)^2 + h^2$$
$$400 = (18-2)^2 + (2r)^2 = 256 + 4r^2$$
$$4r^2 = 144 \implies r = \boxed{6}$$
