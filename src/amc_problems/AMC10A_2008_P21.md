# Problem
A cube with side length 1 is sliced by a plane that passes through two diagonally opposite vertices A and C and the 
midpoints B and D of two opposite edges not containing A or C. What is the area of quadrilateral ABCD?

answers = $\frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}$, $\frac{5}{4}$, $\sqrt{2}$, $\frac{5}{8}$, $\frac{3}{4}$

# Solution

Place the cube with $A=(0,0,0)$ and $C=(1,1,1)$ at diagonally opposite vertices.

**Finding B and D.** The two opposite edges not containing $A$ or $C$ that keep $A,B,C,D$ coplanar are the vertical edges $(1,0,0)\text{–}(1,0,1)$ and $(0,1,0)\text{–}(0,1,1)$. Their midpoints are:

$$B = \left(1,\,0,\,\tfrac{1}{2}\right), \qquad D = \left(0,\,1,\,\tfrac{1}{2}\right)$$

**ABCD is a parallelogram.** Note that $\vec{AB} + \vec{AD} = (1,0,\tfrac12) + (0,1,\tfrac12) = (1,1,1) = \vec{AC}$, confirming the four points are coplanar and $ABCD$ is a parallelogram.

**Area via cross product.**

$$\vec{AB} = \left(1,\,0,\,\tfrac{1}{2}\right), \qquad \vec{AD} = \left(0,\,1,\,\tfrac{1}{2}\right)$$

$$\vec{AB}\times\vec{AD} = \begin{vmatrix}\mathbf{i}&\mathbf{j}&\mathbf{k}\\1&0&\tfrac12\\0&1&\tfrac12\end{vmatrix} = \left(-\tfrac{1}{2},\,-\tfrac{1}{2},\,1\right)$$

$$\text{Area} = \left|\vec{AB}\times\vec{AD}\right| = \sqrt{\tfrac{1}{4}+\tfrac{1}{4}+1} = \sqrt{\tfrac{3}{2}} = \boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{2}}$$

The answer is $\textbf{(A)}\ \dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{2}$.
